# Swirlverse Ops: privacy policy

Swirlverse Ops is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Swirlverse (@theswirlverse) to manage that channel: posting and replying to comments, adding videos to playlists, and reading the channel's own statistics.

- It accesses only the owner's own YouTube channel, with the owner's permission.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal data with anyone.
- Public comment text and channel statistics are used only to manage the channel and are kept in the owner's repository.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
