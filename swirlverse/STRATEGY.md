## Rules of thumb (update when the data disagrees)
1. **Shorts:** 25–40 s. Show the twist on screen within 2 s. Put one CAPS word in the title, ideally with a number.
2. **Titles:** `What If <thing> Was/Had <CAPS twist>? 🌀 #Swirlverse`. Keep them under 60 characters.
3. **Thumbnails** (use thumbkit):
   - 2–4 huge words, one highlighted
   - one big character with an extreme face
   - one hero object with an arrow or burst sticker
   - text in the top 45% of a Short
   - true to the video: curiosity, never fake promises
4. **Explore vs. exploit:** about 70% of runs pick from proven categories (everyday physics, senses/body, planet/earth). About 30% try something new and are logged as an experiment.
5. **Experiments to try, one variable at a time:**
   - hook style: question first vs. shock image first
   - length: about 25 s vs. about 40 s
   - "our world" fact first vs. jumping straight into the parallel world
   - Kiko-led vs. Nova-led
   - number-in-title vs. no number
   - a 2-part series ("Part 2 in my next Short")
   - a "Which world would you pick?" poll ending vs. a question ending
   - topic families: space, body, animals, everyday objects, history-science
6. **Long videos** (every 2 days, 2–4 min, 16:9, chapters ≥ 10 s):
   - Use a big curiosity question everyone has, then the real science, then the parallel world, then the catch, then the good side, then a poll.
   - Each long should also be cut into a Short teaser later. Note it here as an idea; don't make extra uploads.
7. **Never repeat** a topic in history.json, or a close variant of one (e.g. "gravity x3" after "gravity half"), within 60 days.
